The idea behind it all

More ambition.
Less busywork.

We build custom AI employees around a company’s people, knowledge, and tools — giving teams more capacity for what comes next.

01 / Human

People at the center.

Warm surfaces, open space, and everyday language. Technology should feel approachable.

02 / Capable

Clarity over complexity.

Precise typography, purposeful diagrams, and practical examples. Show how the work moves.

03 / Optimistic

A little forward energy.

A bright blue, an upward arrow, and room to imagine. Confident without being loud.

The foundation: the laglabs landing page’s wordmark, Manrope, warm neutrals, and workflow illustrations. The evolution: reference blue replaces orange as the primary accent. This guide codifies usage rules for the team.

01 / Logo & symbol

A small mark.
A clear signature.

Lowercase, confident, and approachable. Our wordmark is the main identifier. The asterisk adds a spark of possibility.

Give it room.

Keep at least 1× the “l” height clear on all sides. Minimum wordmark width: 100px digital / 25mm print. Keep the square dot attached.

A supporting spark.

Use the asterisk for avatars, favicons, and diagrams. Keep the wordmark nearby when introducing the brand. Minimum tile size: 24px.

One color? Keep it simple.

Use the monochrome wordmark for single-ink production. Preserve proportions; avoid shadows, outlines, gradients, or a new icon-wordmark lockup.

Always write laglabs as one lowercase word. Use the supplied outlined SVGs for finished artwork; they do not require a font to be installed.

04 / Design language

Structured.
Never stiff.

Editorial layouts meet useful little systems. Give every idea space, then connect the dots.

01 / A clear starting point

Good ideas need
a little space.

Generous outer marginsLeft-aligned. Always intentional.

Build with rhythm.

Use a 4px base unit. Start with 24px inside cards, 32–48px between groups, and 64–112px between sections. Landing-page gutters scale from 24px to 88px.

Soft edges. Light structure.

Use 1px warm-gray borders. Keep shadows subtle and functional. Corners are modest: 5px for buttons, 8px for cards, 11px for larger workflow panels.

5px8px11px
The icon language

Light, simple, useful.

24 × 24 grid · 1.6px stroke · round caps and joins

Show the work, not the sci-fi.

Use diagrams with cards, thin connectors, dotted grids, and a visible human handoff. Sage identifies people or knowledge; lavender identifies tools. Avoid stock robots, neon glows, and decorative complexity.

Move with purpose.

Use 200ms transitions for controls and a subtle 1–2px arrow shift. Diagram animation should explain a flow. Honor reduced-motion preferences and provide a pause control for continuous motion.

06 / Voice & messaging

Sound like a partner.
Not a pitch.

Lead with human ambition. Explain the technology through the work it helps people do. Be warm, specific, and straightforward.

Our one-sentence introduction

laglabs builds custom AI employees for mid-sized companies — connected to your tools, tailored to your business, and managed by us.

More like usLess like us

“Give your people more room for what’s next.”

“Replace your workforce with autonomous AI.”

“Keep CRM records and follow-ups up to date.”

“Revolutionize your enterprise with next-gen intelligence.”

“Let’s find your first AI employee.”

“Guaranteed 10× productivity. Instantly.”

Warm, not casual

Speak person to person.

Use “you,” “your team,” and “we.” Contractions are welcome. Keep sentences short and jargon explained.

Confident, not inflated

Make the work concrete.

Say what gets done. Use examples, not superlatives. Only publish outcomes, claims, or customer stories you can support.

Optimistic, not magical

Keep people in the picture.

Talk about capacity, judgment, and partnership. Make human review and practical boundaries visible.

Language to build on: AI employees · AI teammates · your people · built around your business · more capacity. Use sentence case. Write AI in capitals and laglabs in lowercase.
